## Intended Motion Grammar

Two neutral mannequin figures begin visibly separated in a stable full-body composition. The initiating figure performs one compact, controlled torso-level push. Contact is brief. The receiving figure shows readable torso displacement and exactly one rear-foot recovery placement, then stabilizes with continued micro-motion. The initiating arm releases and retracts.
