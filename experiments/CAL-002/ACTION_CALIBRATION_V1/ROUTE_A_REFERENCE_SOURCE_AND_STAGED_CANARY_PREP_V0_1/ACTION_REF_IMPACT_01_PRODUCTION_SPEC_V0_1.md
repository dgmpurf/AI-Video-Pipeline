## Intended Motion Grammar

Two neutral mannequin figures begin visibly separated in a stable full-body composition. The initiating figure performs one compact torso-level impact action. Contact is brief. The receiving figure shows immediate readable recoil and exactly one rear-foot step, then stabilizes with continued micro-motion. The initiating limb retracts promptly.
