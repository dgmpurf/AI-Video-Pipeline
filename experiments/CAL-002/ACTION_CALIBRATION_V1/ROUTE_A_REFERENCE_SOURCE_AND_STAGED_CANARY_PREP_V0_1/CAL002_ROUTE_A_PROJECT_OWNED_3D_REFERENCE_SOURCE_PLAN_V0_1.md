# CAL-002 Route A Project-Owned 3D Reference Source Plan V0.1

## Purpose

This plan defines a future rights-safe source strategy for two neutral action-grammar references:

- `ACTION_REF_PUSH_01`
- `ACTION_REF_IMPACT_01`

It is a no-live planning artifact. No model, rig, animation, render, image, video, audio, or other media is created here.

## Preferred Source Strategy

- Strategy ID: `CAL002_ROUTE_A_PROJECT_OWNED_NEUTRAL_3D_ACTION_REFERENCE_SOURCE`
- Preferred rights class: `PROJECT_OWNED_3D_OR_ANIMATED_REFERENCE`

Project-owned neutral 3D is preferred because it can minimize recognizable identity, third-party IP, privacy, performer-consent, and scene-copy risks while giving the project direct control over the motion chain. The cleanest future source would use project-created neutral mannequin meshes, rigs, animation, materials, camera, background, lighting, and render.

No such asset is claimed to exist. No software package or external asset is selected in this phase.

## Ownership And License Requirements

Every component must have documented rights compatible with the intended generative-model input, derivative generation, Provider retention terms, commercial use, and any necessary internal review. A component being free to view or download is not enough.

Required component review:

- mannequin mesh ownership and license;
- rig ownership and license;
- animation ownership and license;
- texture and material ownership and license;
- background and lighting ownership and license;
- software and plugin output rights;
- any mocap or human-performance source and consent;
- generative upload permission;
- derivative-generation permission;
- commercial-use permission;
- Provider-retention permission;
- redistribution permission.

Any unresolved component blocks the reference.

## Disallowed Substitutions

The future reference must not use:

- film, television, anime, game, or commercial clips;
- social-media clips without explicit generative-input rights;
- fan animation or game capture containing third-party IP;
- recognizable fictional characters, environments, props, or branding;
- real-person performance without documented consent;
- traced or copied unlicensed choreography;
- real injury or unsafe-contact footage;
- identifiable faces, private locations, or sensitive data;
- assets whose license conflicts with upload, derivative, retention, or commercial use;
- any material with unresolved provenance.

## Neutral Visual Design

The planned source should use:

- two visually neutral mannequin figures;
- simple, non-distinctive materials;
- no face identity or recognizable body identity;
- no costume language;
- no branded or narrative props;
- a neutral background;
- neutral lighting;
- a static, continuous camera;
- a medium-wide or full-body view;
- both complete bodies and both sets of feet visible;
- readable screen direction;
- no cuts in the useful action window.

The reference is action grammar only. Identity, appearance, scene, camera, composition, lighting, color grade, story, and IP duties remain prohibited.

## Safe Choreography

The action must depict controlled, non-injury contact:

- no weapon;
- no gore;
- no fall;
- no unsafe throw;
- no prolonged pushing or crushing;
- no real harm;
- no aggressive follow-through beyond the compact action family;
- contact is brief and staged;
- both figures remain balanced and recover safely.

The push reference demonstrates a compact push and one rear-foot recovery placement. The impact reference demonstrates a brief torso impact and one rear-foot recoil step.

## Technical Planning Targets

These are local preparation targets, not verified Provider constraints:

- planned container: `MP4` or another later-verified suitable format;
- planned resolution: `1280x720` or `1920x1080`;
- planned aspect ratio: `16:9`;
- planned frame rate: constant `24` or `30` fps;
- planned duration: approximately `2` to `3` seconds;
- planned audio: none;
- planned camera: continuous static medium-wide or full-body;
- planned background: neutral;
- planned figure count: two;
- planned materials: simple and non-distinctive;
- planned lighting: neutral.

Future validation must independently establish accepted input format, file size, duration, frame-rate behavior, upload behavior, and Provider retention terms before any upload authority can exist.

## Reference Review Workflow

1. Obtain separate authority to create exactly the two planned references.
2. Produce the references under documented component rights.
3. Validate the exact files technically.
4. Record byte length and SHA-256 in completed asset records.
5. Review each complete MP4, not only contact sheets or frames.
6. Review rights, provenance, safety, privacy, motion fidelity, and leakage risk.
7. Record a human decision for each exact file.
8. Request separate authorization for Provider-mode revalidation and no-live canary package preparation.
9. Request a fresh exact authorization before any upload or Provider call.

## Future Creation Alternatives

Acceptable future alternatives remain limited to rights-clean sources:

- fully project-created neutral 3D animation;
- contractor-created neutral animation with explicit generative-use rights;
- project-recorded abstract or non-identifying motion with documented consent, only after a separate human decision;
- another project-owned animated technique with equivalent rights and leakage controls.

No alternative is selected here.

## Stopping Conditions

Stop before upload or live preparation if:

- provenance is unresolved;
- any required component right is incompatible;
- a real person lacks documented consent;
- identifiable identity, third-party IP, private data, or real harm is present;
- full bodies or feet are not visible;
- motion family, onset, contact, response, release, or recovery is unclear;
- camera motion, cuts, prolonged contact, or extended-arm freeze is present;
- the human does not approve the exact reference bytes;
- Provider duty separation cannot be verified.

Reference media created in this phase: `false`.
